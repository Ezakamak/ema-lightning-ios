- Canberk Aslan (canberkkkkkk): EMA Lightning model, Apache-2.0.
  https://github.com/canberk7/ema-lightning
  https://huggingface.co/canberkkkkkk/ema-lightning
- Özcan Çelik: EMA Lightning ONNX graphs, tts.js and normalizer.js,
